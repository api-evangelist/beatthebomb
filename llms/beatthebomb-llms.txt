# Beat The Bomb

> Beat The Bomb is an immersive team-based game experience. Teams of 4+ suit up in hazmat gear and race through a gauntlet of interactive game rooms to hack a giant bomb — win and stay clean, lose and get blasted with paint, foam, or slime. Popular for corporate team building, birthday parties, and group events across 7 US locations.

## Experiences
- [Paint Bomb Mission](https://www.beatthebomb.com/games/paint-bomb): Get splattered with paint if you lose!
- [Foam Bomb Mission](https://www.beatthebomb.com/games/foam-bomb): Get covered in foam if you lose!
- [Slime Bomb Mission](https://www.beatthebomb.com/games/slime-bomb): Get slimed if you lose!
- [Cash Bomb](https://www.beatthebomb.com/games/cash-bomb): The final mission. You’ve earned the right. Now go win $1,000.
- [Arcade Bomb Battle](https://www.beatthebomb.com/games/arcade-bomb-battle): Competitive arcade gaming with an explosive twist!
